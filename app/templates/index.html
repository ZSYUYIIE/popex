Local, private, free

Turn a specific recording into a reviewable draft score.

Add local audio or video, or a supported video link. PopEx analyses timing and key, can separate stems, transcribes notes and drum hits, suggests chord symbols and tablature, and builds a draft score you can review against the recording, correct, and export as MusicXML or MIDI.

Results are AI-assisted drafts with honest warnings, not publication-ready engraving. Different recordings of the same song stay separate versions with their own scores.

Current workflow

  1. Prepare and analyseKeep the source, create analysis audio, estimate tempo, meter, tuning, and key.
  2. TranscribeOptionally separate stems, then detect notes, drum hits, parts, and harmony.
  3. Build the scoreMeasures, chord symbols, drum part, and guitar or bass tablature.
  4. Review and correctPlay each bar, fix mistakes, and export MusicXML or MIDI.
  5. Group versionsFile studio, live, and other versions under one song and compare them.
