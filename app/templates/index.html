How it works

Every stage runs on demand, on your machine, and keeps its own results.

  1. PrepareKeep the source, make analysis audio, estimate tempo, meter, tuning, and key.
  2. SeparateOptionally split vocals, bass, drums, and accompaniment with a local model.
  3. TranscribeDetect notes and drum hits, group parts, and suggest harmony.
  4. NotateBars, chord symbols, a drum part, tabs, key, and separate parts.
  5. ReviewPlay each bar, fix what you hear, undo freely, export MusicXML or MIDI.
  6. CompareGroup studio and live versions, then compare them side by side.

Results are drafts with honest warnings, not publication-ready engraving. When the audio cannot support precision, PopEx says so instead of guessing.
